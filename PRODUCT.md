# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Small business owners and founders — established companies, consultancies, architectural/professional firms, and founders launching a new service or campaign — who need a credible, professional website and don't have in-house design/dev capacity. They arrive needing one of: a multi-page business site, a focused landing page for a launch, a redesign of an outdated/slow existing site, or a custom interactive feature (booking, intake, configurator).

## Product Purpose

Wibsity is a web design/dev studio that builds fixed-scope websites for these clients on a fast, predictable timeline (3–5 days for standard sites, 5–7+ for advanced custom builds), with the scope, timeline, and price agreed in writing before work starts. Success is a live site the client can point to as evidence of competence and that reliably captures inquiries/leads for their business.

## Positioning

Every site is hand-coded with real React/TypeScript rather than assembled on template page-builders (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress themes). This is the mechanism behind the studio's concrete claims — faster load times, a codebase that stays maintainable as the client's needs grow, and no proprietary-platform lock-in — none of which a template-based competitor can truthfully claim. This positioning is already expressed on the homepage (the Studio Manifesto section, including a real excerpted code panel from this repo's own `Button.tsx`).

## Operating Context

Engagement runs as: initial discovery conversation → fixed-scope written proposal (timeline + price) → build sprint → handover. Communication is direct with the founder/engineer-designer building the site, not routed through an account manager. Deployment targets modern hosting (Vercel/Netlify) with CDN + automated SSL; a headless CMS is integrated when the client needs to self-edit content later. Primary contact channels are WhatsApp and a 4-step "Start a Project" inquiry form (name → phone → budget → project type), both wired throughout the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four service lines, each with its own scope type: Business Websites (multi-page), Landing Pages (single-page, conversion-focused), Website Redesigns & Modernization (audit + rebuild of an existing site), and Custom Web Experiences (booking flows, configurators, client portals, third-party API/webhook integrations).
- Fixed-scope pricing is a hard commitment — no hidden fees, scope agreed before start.
- Domain is always registered in the client's name and is theirs to keep/transfer (a real, positive ownership claim the site makes).
- The studio does **not** transfer code/IP ownership of its own reusable components/tooling to clients — this is a deliberate, existing content guardrail (see CLAUDE.md) and must not be contradicted by future copy.
- No numeric/unverified performance claims (e.g. "<500ms load") may be made without a real measurement behind them; "Fast Loading"/"Accessible by Default" (WCAG AA) are the standing qualitative claims in current use.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Wibsity. Brand accent color extracted from the existing logo mark (`#4B50FE` family — see CLAUDE.md Styling section for the full token set); this is an existing, binding visual constraint, not open for reinterpretation without a deliberate redesign decision.
- Voice is direct and confident, oriented at business-outcome language for the buyer (not developer-facing process pride) — reflected in the homepage manifesto's copy reframe from "no templates, no slow builders" toward "built to load fast, stay maintainable, and never lock you in."

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. There are no real client sites, testimonials, or case studies to reference — future design and content work must not fabricate any client names, testimonials, metrics, or case studies. All trust-building claims must trace back to real, already-stated facts (turnaround time, fixed-scope process, founder-direct contact, WCAG AA accessibility work actually done).

## Product Principles

1. Fixed scope and price agreed in writing before work starts — no surprises, no hidden fees.
2. Fast, predictable turnaround (3–5 days standard, 5–7+ for custom) via focused sprints and direct founder communication.
3. Real hand-coded engineering (React/TypeScript, not template builders) as the substance behind every speed/maintainability/no-lock-in claim.
4. Never fabricate trust signals — value claims must trace to real, current facts, not invented metrics or testimonials.
5. Never claim client transfer of Wibsity's own underlying code/IP; domain ownership is the one ownership claim the site makes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Every build targets WCAG AA contrast and full keyboard navigation from day one, treated as a real, checkable claim rather than a retrofit — this is already implemented practice on the current site (see CLAUDE.md's Accessibility conventions section) and should be preserved in future work.
